# 801 Labs Vulnerability Disclosure & Bug Bounty Program

801 Labs welcomes responsible security research and encourages researchers to report vulnerabilities affecting infrastructure, websites, applications, or systems operated by 801 Labs.

### Scope

Systems explicitly operated and authorized for testing by 801 Labs, including `801labs.org` and its subdomains, unless otherwise excluded.

### Responsible Disclosure

- Report vulnerabilities to security@801labs.org.
- Avoid accessing, modifying, or destroying other users' data.
- Do not conduct denial-of-service attacks, social engineering, or physical intrusion.
- Allow reasonable time for remediation before public disclosure.
- Limit testing to systems that 801 Labs owns or has explicitly authorized for testing.

### Bug Bounties

801 Labs may offer monetary rewards for eligible, independently verified security vulnerabilities.

Reward amounts, eligibility, and payment availability are determined by 801 Labs and must be confirmed with the program administrators.

Submission does not guarantee payment.

### Response Commitments

801 Labs aims to acknowledge reports within seven calendar days and provide periodic status updates as remediation progresses.

### Safe Harbor

801 Labs supports good-faith, authorized security research conducted in accordance with this policy. Research outside the published scope is not authorized by this policy.
