# Digital Dusk FAQ

## What is Digital Dusk?

Digital Dusk is a hacker party run by 801 Labs during SAINTCON. Digital Dusk features art, interactive exhibits, a bar, a DJ list, and other fun visual/interactive things.

## When is Digital Dusk?

Digital Dusk is held on the Thursday night of the SAINTCON week. Unfortunately, it will conflict with SAINTCON’s family night.

## Is Digital Dusk a public event?

Digital Dusk is a private event that you must RSVP to attend. Private and public events are legally distinct, and come with very different requirements and expectations. We do not stop any attendee from RSVPing on-site. Digital Dusk is not open to the public \- it is for SAINTCON attendees and their friends and family.

## Can I bring a friend?

Absolutely, as many as you like\! Just ensure that each person has RSVP’d to the event. They may RSVP at the door as well.

## Is Digital Dusk free?

Attending 801 Lab's SAINTCON party will always be free. In order to subsidize the cost of running the event, 801 Labs will run various fundraising activities both at SAINTCON and throughout the year. Digital Dusk is funded by 801 Labs via:

* Year round:  
  * Donations from the community on our [Every.org](http://Every.org) platform or in cash  
  * Corporate matching and giving programs  
  * Purchasing badges or merchandise from 801 Labs  
* At the 801 Labs SAINTCON booth:  
  * Donations (cash or credit) during the conference  
  * Purchasing badges, minibadges, minibadge displays, or merchandise from 801 Labs during the conference  
* At Digital Dusk  
  * Tipping your bartenders  
  * Donating online or in person

## How many attend Digital Dusk?

In 2025 we had 250\~ RSVPs, and 150\~ attendees. We will be setting a 150 person attendance cap in 2026\.

## Does Digital Dusk carry insurance?

801 Labs carries event liability and injury insurance for the duration of the event.

## Does Digital Dusk need permits?

As long as Digital Dusk remains a private event, we are not required to pull any permit for events or alcohol.

## How old do you have to be to attend Digital Dusk?

* 18+, with a special wristband to identify that you are not of legal age to consume alcohol. You may not remove this wristband during the event.  
* 21+ if you wish to drink. You must bring and provide valid identification to acquire a wristband/hand stamp that will allow you to drink.

## Is alcohol provided?

Alcohol is provided, and it's free\! We just ask that you tip your hard-working bartenders. Attendees who wish to consume alcohol must have their ID checked and receive a wristband or a hand stamp. You will be required to show your wristband/hand stamp whenever you get a drink from the bar. Any attendee who is drinking without a wristband or a hand stamp will be required to show ID \- if you fail this check (such as “my ID is in my other pants\!”), you will be asked to leave the venue immediately.

## What if I don’t drink?

We understand that many people do not consume alcohol, and they are more than welcome at Digital Dusk\! If you do not wish to drink, you do not have to acquire a wristband. Chilled canned sodas and water are provided for free to all of our attendees.

## What if I get hungry?

Digital Dusk will have a food truck on site, though you must pay for food yourself.

## Do we have sponsors?

Digital Dusk is run by 801 Labs, a nonprofit hackerspace out of Salt Lake City Utah. If you wish to discuss the possibility of sponsoring Digital Dusk, please email `kimber` at this domain.

## What goes on at Digital Dusk?

* A gathering of nerds, makers, hackers, code-breakers, artists, and like-minded individuals  
* Live EDM DJs, with lighting, fog machine, projectors etc  
* Fun interactive exhibits, digital and analog art, projections, and more  
* Free open bar, with spirits, mixers, beer, wine, and seltzers  
* Free soda bar and water station
* Food truck

# Code of Conduct

## What we expect from you:

* To be excellent to each other, your event hosts, and the venue  
* To be respectful of other attendees and our venue space \- including the equipment, facilities, staff, fixtures, etc.  
* To manage your alcohol intake, and to arrive and depart safely

## What will get you turned away at the door:

* Outside alcoholic beverages. This is not a BYOB event  
* Clothing (or lack of) that could violate any local or state laws  
  * No shirt, no shoes, no entry  
* Weapons  
* Not RSVPing \- even your \+1s need to RSVP\!  
* Arriving visually intoxicated

## What will get you kicked out:

* Disrespectful behavior towards people or property
* Violence, threats of violence, or violent language  
* Unwelcome touching or conversation, sexual language, or verbal abuse  
* Damaging or tampering with property \- such as exhibits, equipment, venue, or personal property. This includes:  
  * Hacking (the venue, staff, or other attendees)  
  * Unauthorized access to rooms or systems  
  * Vandalism  
  * Stickers  
  * Unplugging things  
  * Pressing buttons  
  * Moving things  
* Theft of property  
* Becoming too intoxicated to participate safely  
* Consuming alcohol without a wristband or proof of 21+  
* Consuming illegal drugs 
* Sneaking in or consuming outside alcohol  
* Providing alcohol to attendees without a wristband
