# Code of Conduct

## What we expect from you

* To be excellent to each other, your event hosts, and the venue  
* To be respectful of other attendees and our venue space \- including the equipment, facilities, staff, fixtures, etc.  
* To manage your alcohol intake, and to arrive and depart safely

## What will get you turned away at the door

* Outside alcoholic beverages. This is not a BYOB event  
* Clothing (or lack of) that could violate any local or state laws  
  * No shirt, no shoes, no entry  
* Weapons  
* Not RSVPing \- even your \+1s need to RSVP\!  
* Arriving visually intoxicated

## What will get you kicked out

* Disrespectful behavior towards people or property
* Violence, threats of violence, or violent language  
* Trespassing into off-limits areas of the venue 
* Unwelcome touching or conversation, sexual language, or verbal abuse  
* Damaging or tampering with property \- such as exhibits, equipment, venue, or personal property. This includes:  
  * Hacking (the venue, staff, or other attendees)  
  * Unauthorized access to rooms or systems  
  * Vandalism  
  * Stickers  
  * Unplugging things  
  * Pressing buttons  
  * Moving things  
* Theft of property  
* Becoming too intoxicated to participate safely  
* Consuming alcohol without a wristband or proof of 21+  
* Consuming illegal drugs 
* Sneaking in or consuming outside alcohol  
* Providing alcohol to attendees without a wristband
